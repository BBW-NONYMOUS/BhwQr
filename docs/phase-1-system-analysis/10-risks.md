> **Role speaking: System Analyst**

# 10 – Risk Register

This register lists the risks that could stop the BHW QR Attendance System from being accurate, secure, private, usable or delivered on time at the Barangay Kalawag II Health Workers Office. Each risk has an owner from the five project roles, a mitigation that points to the requirement or decision that carries it out (FR-xx in `02-functional-requirements.md`, NFR-xx in `03-non-functional-requirements.md`, D-xx in `00-assumptions-and-decisions.md`), and a residual rating after the mitigation is in place.

All times are Asia/Manila.

## 1. Rating method

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Likelihood (L/M/H) | L = unlikely during the first year of use; M = may happen a few times a year; H = expected to happen, or already happens with the manual logbook. |
| Impact (L/M/H) | L = small inconvenience, fixed the same day; M = wrong or missing records for some employees, or a feature unavailable for up to a day; H = falsified or lost attendance, a personal data breach, legal non-acceptance, or the system cannot be used. |
| Inherent score | Likelihood × Impact with L = 1, M = 2, H = 3. Score 6–9 = **High**, 3–4 = **Medium**, 1–2 = **Low**. |
| Residual | The expected level (L/M/H) after the mitigation is in place. A residual of M means the risk is accepted and watched with the early-warning indicators in section 4. |
| Owner role | The role that carries out and tracks the mitigation: System Administrator, System Analyst, Software Engineer, Frontend Designer / Developer, or Backend Developer. |

Categories: **Security**, **Privacy/Legal**, **Technical**, **Operational**, **Adoption**, **Schedule**.
